Each criterion's maximum equals its weight (30% = 30 points), so every category totals 100. Scores save automatically and appear on every judge's screen.

Category

TOTAL = SUM(criteria) per judge · AVERAGE = AVERAGE(judge totals) · RANK = RANK.EQ(average, all averages, 0), so the highest average ranks 1 and ties share a rank. A candidate is ranked once every judge has finished scoring them.

Standings

OVERALL = rank of AVERAGE(category ranks), lowest first. If two candidates tie, the one with the higher average score across categories places higher.
